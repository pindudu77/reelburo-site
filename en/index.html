faq

FAQ

Why is this better than a regular shoot?

Speed, consistency and almost none of your time and effort.
No camera, no lighting, no retakes.
A video ships 48 hours after script approval, and the face and voice stay the same across the feed.

How are you different from content farms?

Content farms run a template pipeline with a one-to-two-week setup.
We write scripts for your product, approve them before editing and include a revision per video.
No onboarding: a 30-minute brief, and the video ships 48 hours after the script.

Doesn't it look cheap?

We approve the avatar look and delivery with you before production.
Your own avatar built from your face and voice, or a ready one from the catalog.
Everything is made to your liking.

How long until the first video?

48 hours after script approval, and the script arrives the day of the brief.
After that we work on a schedule, usually a batch of videos every week.

Any hidden fees?

No.
The package price is fixed at the brief and depends on the video format.
Inside: AI-tool subscriptions, avatar licensing, voice-over, design and a revision per video.
You only pay extra if you ask for more, like additional revisions.

I already have footage. Will you do editing only?

Yes.
Send us your phone footage and you get back a fully edited and designed video, same 48-hour turnaround.
Package prices stay the same: we charge per video, not per format.

I have no footage and I do not want to film. What then?

Then you do not have to film at all.
Material is almost always there: a channel, a site, a price list, the questions clients ask you every week. We turn that into scripts.
On camera there is a real person nobody filmed, an AI presenter picked to match your niche and approved by you before production.
The main format is your own twin: your face and your voice, from a single phone recording.

Do you post the videos for us?

Usually we deliver finished files and you post them in a minute: no account access, no passwords.
There is also a posting option: we publish through a scheduling service, still no passwords needed.
Discussed at the brief.

How does payment work?

The first package: 50% upfront, 50% on delivery.
After that packages are paid upfront, by card or transfer, details on Telegram.
No auto-charges: you confirm each month yourself.

What if the format doesn't land?

That is what Starter is for: 3 videos in a week, paid 50/50.
Not a fit? You simply do not continue, no obligations.
We will still revise the first video either way.

How many revisions are included?

One revision per video, two on Studio.
More by agreement.
Usually it is not needed: scripts are approved before editing.

Which languages?

Almost any: the avatar speaks more than 175 languages.
Most of our work is Russian and English.
One script can ship in several languages at once, no accent.

Is this legal?

Yes. We use licensed avatars: the people behind them gave the service consent for commercial use. Platforms allow this content. Where an "AI-made" label is required, we add it. It is a platform rule, not a ban.

Can it be my face and voice?

Yes, that is the main format: the avatar is built from your face and voice.
You record yourself in the service following our guide and confirm consent on camera right there.
Likeness is tuned with your photos and a voice note; treat the first video as a fitting, a couple of revisions is normal.
Do not want to film at all? We will pick a ready avatar from the catalog.

Won't the algorithms bury AI videos?

Platforms downrank boring, not "AI": weak hooks and flat delivery.
That is why we approve every script before editing and obsess over the first three seconds.
We guarantee steady production and quality.
On Studio, we review your numbers monthly and change what is not working.

Who owns the videos?

You do. Post them anywhere, as much as you want, with no restrictions and no licensing fees.
