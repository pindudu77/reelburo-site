24/7 reply desk for a real estate agency in Dubai

Enquiries on Instagram, Facebook and Telegram arrive around the clock, and the broker replies when free. One lost enquiry for a million-dirham apartment costs more than a year of subscription. Here every message gets an answer in the broker's voice: within a minute, in English or Russian, to the point.

What happens when a client writes

The client asks about price, area, mortgage or a viewing and gets an answer in their language within a minute.

The machine asks three questions, one per message: budget, purpose (living or investment), timing.

When the client is ready to view, it asks when it suits them and books it.

The broker gets a card: who, budget, purpose, timing, viewing time. From there the broker leads.

What the head of the agency sees

Every evening: how many enquiries, how many replies, who is waiting.

Every week: enquiries, replies within a minute, qualified, viewings booked, average reply at night.

Cards go to the agent who runs the area or project, by keywords in the chat.

Old unanswered chats from the last 30 days are found automatically, with what they cost.

What the machine does not do

It does not quote prices or yields for the broker: figures for a unit come from a person.

It does not message strangers first on Instagram and Facebook: it replies to those who wrote or commented.

It does not argue or push: a refusal is respected with silence.

It does not run the deal: after a viewing is booked the agent leads the chat.

Night shift

A starter plan: the desk answers only from 8 pm to 9 am and on weekends, when more than half of enquiries arrive and nobody answers. You handle the day. Switch to full coverage any day.

Frequently asked questions

Which language does it reply in?

The client's: English or Russian, detected from the message.

What if the client asks something not covered?

The machine calls the agent: a card with the question arrives on Telegram and a person answers.

Can several agents be connected?

Yes. In the cabinet you list a name, Telegram and the area or project keywords: enquiries with those words go to that agent, the rest to the one marked «*».

What about WhatsApp?

It is connected after Meta business verification. Until then Instagram, Facebook and Telegram.

How do I see what the machine replies?

The cabinet shows every chat, and the reply texts are visible and editable before launch.
